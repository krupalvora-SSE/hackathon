04 What Krupal and Aarti do

They call customers whose service is due and book the slot Beat Planning recommends

One call covers the cases our schedulers handle every day. Every call ends with a clear outcome and a note on the site.

Confirms the recommended slot

Opens with the date Beat Planning picked and books it on a yes.

"Friday, 5 December ko slot hai, kya aap available rahenge?"

Offers other slots

Three-hour windows (9–12, 12–3, 3–6) on the same day, or other days with free slots in Beat Planning.

"Friday ya Saturday mein se koi din theek rahega?"

Asks for site details

Checks the A-type ladder, a water source and a light connection before booking, so the hero can do the job.

"Kya aapke paas A-type ladder hai?"

Puts a site on hold

If the site isn't ready, they get a date it will be and the customer's OK to pause calls until then.

"Ladder kab tak arrange ho jayegi?"

Shows empathy

When a customer is going through a hard time, they slow down, apologise, stop the call and ask a senior to follow up later.

"Yeh sunkar bahut dukh hua. Abhi hum call nahi karenge."

Writes the call notes

After every call they post a summary and the structured call record as a comment on the site in Beat Planning.

Outcome, slot, readiness, hold date and callback time

Keeps learning, call after call

They only need a teacher. Someone reviews calls in the Questions view, finds where the script failed, and writes the right answer. The bot loads it at the start of the next call.

08 How it's built

Three things we designed for

While designing the solution, we focused on three aspects in particular.

01

Cost efficiency

Open-source STT and TTS run alongside managed AI services. That keeps operating cost down and reduces vendor dependency, with the same architecture either way.

Open source: ₹0.3 a call
02

Contextual awareness and empathy

The agent goes beyond a script. It understands the customer's context and emotional state, responds with empathy, and adapts or steps away when the situation calls for it.

Sensitive moment: stop and hand to a senior
03

Continuous learning

A feedback-driven learning module spots conversations that go off track, captures the knowledge gap, and lets the agent improve with every piece of human feedback.

Questions view: answer once, used from the next call

Natural language at the edge. Deterministic state and validation at the core.

Identify→Offer slot→Check readiness→Recap→Confirm→Record
09 Architecture

Two call channels share one audio pipeline

The app coordinates calls and shows outcomes to operators. The bot runs a streaming pipeline with the flow engine in the middle.

Channel

Browser

The dashboard uses the app API. Browser mic audio connects straight to the bot over WebRTC at 16 kHz.

Channel

Phone

The app starts an Exotel call. Exotel streams the customer's phone audio to the bot over a WebSocket at 8 kHz.

Bookings, call coordination, Exotel dial requests, call logs and live dashboard updates.

10 Two layers

The AI understands the customer. The flow controls the service.

Keeping these apart means the LLM never makes a booking decision on its own.

Language layer

LLM + speech

  • Transcribes Hindi and Hinglish speech
  • Classifies each turn into an allowed intent
  • Extracts dates, time bands, reasons and readiness
  • Reports the turn through a constrained tool
  • Uses approved FAQ answers when available
Workflow layer

Flow engine

  • Checks the intent against the current state
  • Evaluates code-owned guards and policies
  • Runs only configured actions
  • Validates and records outcomes
  • Speaks reviewed prompts for critical steps

12 Safety and scope

What is safe by design, and what is still a prototype

Safety and data
  • Phone calls only go to an allowlist of test numbers
  • The greeting says the call is recorded
  • A do-not-call request ends the call
  • No promises on prices, savings or subsidy
  • Voice cloning needs consent before upload
  • Cloud speech and LLM send audio or text to providers; local IndicConformer is an STT alternative
Prototype today
  • Bookings are seeded from JSON and held in memory
  • Call logs are saved locally, with a result backup from the bot
  • Beat Plan / Zeus actions use a mock; real API contracts are pending
  • Outcomes, cost and latency are instrumented; no performance result is claimed yet
  • A hackathon prototype, not a production dialer
Before production: connect and validate the real service APIs, add durable storage, review provider data retention, and run controlled evaluations. Credentials stay in git-ignored environment files.
