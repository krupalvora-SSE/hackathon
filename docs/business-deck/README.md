# Krupal business deck

One-page pitch for the hackathon (SSE-15567). Business first: team, problem, Krupal and Aarti, what they do, where they sit, cost per call and the funnel calculator. Then how it is built: architecture, the two layers, operator console, safety and scope, demo.

Open `index.html` in a browser. Everything is in the one file (logo embedded); fonts load from Google Fonts.

Calculator numbers are Pan India: sites due are Oct 2026 PM visits (SSE + NSSE); in-app and WhatsApp conversion rates are derived from Bengaluru + Lucknow outreach in Nov 2026; scheduler rates are the team target; AI calling rates are assumptions.
